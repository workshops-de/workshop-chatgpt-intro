# Which plan do you need?

::left::

**For yourself**

- **Free** — enough to find out whether this fits your work
- **Plus** — the sweet spot for most people who use it daily

::right::

**For a company**

- **Business / Enterprise** — your data is not used for training by default, plus admin controls and single sign-on

::bottom::
<Callout type="important">
The plan is not only about speed and limits. It changes what happens to the text you type.
</Callout>

# Choosing how hard it thinks

The picker no longer asks which brain you want. It asks how much thinking you are willing to pay for.

- **Instant** — the default, answers immediately, right for most everyday requests
- **Reasoning levels** — the model works through the problem before answering, in exchange for time
- On paid plans it can switch up automatically when a request looks complex

Raise it for analysis, comparisons, planning and anything with several steps. Leave it low for rewriting an email.

<Callout type="warning">
Model names and plan limits change every few months. Trust the picker in front of you, not a screenshot from a workshop.
</Callout>

# Custom instructions

Written once, applied to every new chat. This is the highest-value five minutes of the day.

Worth putting in:

- What you do, so it stops explaining your own field to you
- Who your typical audience is
- How you want answers: length, tone, language, whether to ask before assuming

A good starting line: *Be concise. If my request is ambiguous, ask before you answer.*

# Memory and projects

::left::

**Memory**

Carries details across chats so you stop repeating yourself.

Review it now and then. It also remembers things you would rather it forgot.

::right::

**Projects**

A folder with a brain: related chats, reference files and their own instructions in one place.

Project-only memory keeps sensitive work sealed off from the rest.

::bottom::
<Callout type="info">
Rule of thumb: anything you will come back to more than twice belongs in a project.
</Callout>

# Data controls

This is the slide to take back to your organisation.

- On personal plans, your conversations are used to improve the models **by default**
- You can switch that off under Settings, Data Controls, "Improve the model for everyone"
- On Business, Enterprise and Edu plans, training is off by default and the company owns the data
- Temporary chat leaves no trace in your history and no trace in memory

<Callout type="important">
Switching off training does not mean nothing is stored. Conversations are still retained for a period for abuse monitoring.
</Callout>

---

# Before you paste anything

Ask one question: would I be comfortable if this text appeared outside the company?

- Customer names, personal data and health information stay out unless your plan and your policy allow it
- Passwords, keys and access tokens never go in, on any plan
- Unpublished figures belong in a company plan, not a private account

<Callout type="warning">
Check your own organisation's rules before the workshop ends. "Nobody told me" is not a defence anyone accepts.
</Callout>
