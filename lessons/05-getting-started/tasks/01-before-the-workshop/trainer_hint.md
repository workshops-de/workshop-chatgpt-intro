## Purpose

Removes the single biggest time sink from the first hands-on block. Without this task, registration reliably eats eight to ten minutes of the Lesson 5 exercise, and the people still signing up miss the walkthrough.

## When to Send It

Send the link with the joining instructions, at least three days before the workshop. One reminder the day before measurably improves the completion rate.

For inhouse workshops, send step 3 to the client contact as well, not just to the participants. The question of which account people may use is usually decided above their level, and the answer changes how you run Lessons 5 to 7.

## What to Expect on the Day

Assume that roughly a quarter of participants will not have done it. Plan for a five-minute buffer at the start of Lesson 5 and pair anyone still registering with a neighbour so they can follow along in the meantime.

Step 3 is the one people skip most often. Ask about it explicitly at the start of the day, because the answers shape how frank you can be in the data controls section.

## Signals Worth Reading

- **Many participants on personal accounts in an inhouse workshop** — the data controls slide in Lesson 5 becomes the most important slide of the day
- **Nobody knows whether internal documents may be uploaded** — expect the file-based exercises in Lesson 7 to need a substitute example
- **The company blocks the site entirely** — clarify with the client before the workshop whether it will be unblocked for the day, otherwise the whole afternoon needs replanning
