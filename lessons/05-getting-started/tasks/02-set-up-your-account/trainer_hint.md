## Purpose

The first hands-on exercise of the day. Beyond the settings, the goal is that everyone has typed something and seen it work, so the afternoon is not the first contact. Step 1 comes before everything else on purpose, so nobody pastes company material before deciding what happens to it.

## Preparation Before the Workshop

**Check this the week before every delivery.** Menu labels, plan names and reasoning options change several times a year. Walk through all five steps yourself in a free account and, if you have one, a company account.

Ask the client in advance whether participants will have company accounts and whether admins have locked settings. On a locked workspace, steps 1 and 2 look completely different, and discovering that live costs ten minutes.

## Timing

| Phase | Minutes |
|---|---|
| Steps 1 and 2, guided together at the front | 8 |
| Steps 3 to 5, independent | 10 |
| Questions | 2 |

Run steps 1 and 2 as a walkthrough with your own screen shared. Beginners lose more time hunting for a menu than doing the actual task.

## Common Issues

**Account creation eats the exercise.** The preparation task exists to prevent exactly this, but expect a quarter of the room to have skipped it. Pair anyone still registering with a neighbour rather than holding up the group.

**Different interfaces in the room.** Free, Plus and company accounts differ, and rollouts are gradual. Say this at the start so people stop assuming they did something wrong.

**Someone pastes real customer data into the first test.** This is the moment to intervene visibly and without embarrassing them. It is exactly the reflex the day is meant to build.

**Custom instructions come out as one vague word.** Walk the room and ask people to read theirs aloud. Quality rises immediately when someone else will hear it.

## Discussion Points

- What did your data controls turn out to be set to, and did that surprise you?
- Who in your organisation has actually decided this?
- What did it remember about you that you had not intended?

## Bridge to the Next Lesson

Point out that the setup is now done and the results are still uneven across the room. That is the perfect setup for Lesson 6: the remaining difference is not the tool, it is the request.
