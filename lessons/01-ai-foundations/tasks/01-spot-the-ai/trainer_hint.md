## Purpose

This exercise anchors the abstract definitions in the participants' own reality before ChatGPT is even mentioned. Everything that follows in the workshop builds on the distinction between written rules and learned patterns, and on the insight that a model fails quietly outside its training data.

## Timing

| Phase | Minutes |
|---|---|
| Individual work (steps 1 to 4) | 8 |
| Sharing in the group (step 5) | 5 |
| Wrap-up by the trainer | 2 |

For a remote session, use breakout rooms of three people for the individual phase and collect results in the main room.

## Common Issues

**Participants get stuck on the first step.** Some people claim they use no software worth mentioning. Prompt them with their private life instead: streaming recommendations, photo apps, navigation. The point lands either way.

**The classification turns into hair-splitting.** Some products genuinely combine several learning types. Cut this off early with the message that the reasoning matters, not the label. If a debate runs long, park it and come back to it in Lesson 02, where ChatGPT itself turns out to use all three.

**Someone insists that everything is "just statistics".** They are not wrong, and it is a good moment to agree openly. Modern AI is statistics at enormous scale. Saying this out loud builds more credibility than defending the marketing term.

**Someone insists that AI "understands".** Do not settle this in the discussion round. Note it visibly and promise the answer for Lesson 04, where tokens and next-word prediction make the point far better than an argument can.

## Discussion Points

- Which of your examples were you surprised to find on the list?
- Where would you not want a learned system deciding, even if it were more accurate than a human?
- Who is responsible when a learned system gets it wrong?

## Bridge to the Next Lesson

Close with the observation that everything collected here does exactly one narrow job. Then ask what changes when a single system can be pointed at almost any text task. That is the entry point into Lesson 02.
