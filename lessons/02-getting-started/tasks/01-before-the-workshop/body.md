## Overview

Please complete this **before the workshop starts**. It takes about ten minutes and it protects the hands-on time on the day: every minute spent registering during the workshop is a minute nobody spends practising.

You do not need a paid plan. A free account is enough for everything we do.

## Steps

### 1. Create an account or check that yours works

Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com), sign up or log in, and send one message to confirm you get an answer.

If your company provides an account, use that one rather than a private account. Which account you bring changes what you are allowed to type into it during the exercises.

### 2. Find out which plan you are on

Open your profile menu and look at the plan shown there. Note it down. Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise — any of them is fine, but you should know which one you brought.

### 3. Clarify your company's rules

Answer these two questions before you arrive, or find out who can answer them:

- Am I allowed to use ChatGPT for work at all, and on which account?
- Am I allowed to paste or upload internal documents?

If the honest answer is "nobody has decided this", write that down. It is a legitimate result and several exercises will refer back to it.

### 4. If you are joining remotely

Check that you can share your screen in the meeting tool. Some exercises are easier to help with when a trainer can see what you see.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Account works and has answered at least one message
- [ ] You know which plan you are on
- [ ] You know whether you may use it for work, or that nobody has decided
- [ ] For remote sessions, screen sharing has been tested

## Troubleshooting

**"Registration asks for a phone number."**
That is normal for new personal accounts. If you would rather not provide one, ask whether your company can give you an account instead.

**"My company blocks the site."**
Do not try to work around it. Note it down and bring it to the workshop. It is a relevant fact about your situation, and the exercises can be adapted.

**"I already have an account but have not used it in a year."**
Log in anyway and check it still works. The interface has changed considerably, and so has what is available on your plan.
