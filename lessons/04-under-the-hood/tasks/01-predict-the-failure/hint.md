<details>
<summary>💡 Hint 1: Sorting the eight quickly</summary>

Ask one question per request: **where do the facts come from?**

- From the text I attached → reliable
- From the model's memory of the internet → risky
- From this week → impossible without a tool
- From inside my company → impossible unless I supply it

</details>

<details>
<summary>💡 Hint 2: The letter-counting one</summary>

"strawberry" does not reach the model as ten letters. It arrives as a small number of tokens, and the letters inside them are not something it can inspect.

This is why a system that discusses philosophy can miscount letters in a short word. It is not a sign of stupidity, it is a sign that you misunderstood what it receives.

</details>

<details>
<summary>💡 Hint 3: How to repair a failing request</summary>

Three moves, and you almost always need only one:

- **Add material** — paste the competitor's press release instead of asking what they announced
- **Add a tool** — turn on web search, or attach the file
- **Narrow the question** — ask what to look for in the literature, rather than for specific studies

</details>

<details>
<summary>💡 Hint 4: The pattern in the reliable ones</summary>

They all hand over the material and ask for a transformation of it.

Nothing has to be remembered, looked up or counted. Everything needed is already in the message.

That is the whole rule, and it carries you through the rest of the day.

</details>
